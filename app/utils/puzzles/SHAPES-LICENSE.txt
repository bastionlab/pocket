shapes.ts: country silhouettes for the Shape Guess game, generated from the "world-atlas" npm package, version 2.0.2
(countries-50m.json, https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas), whose data comes from Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com).

Processing: TopoJSON decoded, each country projected (equirectangular scaled by cos of its mean latitude, longitudes
shifted across the antimeridian for Russia / Fiji), distant overseas territories and slivers under 0.8 % of the largest
piece left out, simplified with Douglas-Peucker, written as one SVG path per country. Countries under 9,000 km2 are
left out (no recognisable shape). The generator script is not part of the add-on.
